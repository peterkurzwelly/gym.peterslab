# 12-week program · Consistency first

Start: Monday 9 November 2026 · End: Sunday 31 January 2027

## The one goal

**12 weeks without a zero week.** Fitness follows from that. Every rule below exists to protect it.

## The weekly deal

| Level | What it means | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| **Target** | 3 strength sessions + 1 cardio session | A full week |
| **Floor** | 2 strength sessions, 30 minute versions are fine | A full week, streak intact |
| **Bonus** | 4th strength session or an extra easy cardio | Extra, never required |

There are no fixed days. You always do **the next session in line** (A → B → C → A …) whenever it fits. Cardio goes anywhere in the week.

## Five rules

1. **Hit the floor first.** Book two sessions in your calendar every Sunday, as if they were meetings. Those two are the week. The rest is upside.
2. **Never miss twice.** One missed session is normal. After a miss, the next session happens within 48 hours, even as a 30 minute version.
3. **Short beats skipped.** Only 30 minutes? Do the short version (marked ★ below). It counts.
4. **Coming back is easy on purpose.** After a zero week, the first session back drops one set per exercise and keeps the same weights. No catching up.
5. **Never stack sessions.** A missed day is gone. Don't double up.

**Sensible spacing:** avoid strength three days in a row, and keep hard cardio at least a day away from session A or B, the sessions with the heaviest leg work.

## The three phases

| Weeks | Phase | Sets per main exercise | Effort (reps in reserve, RIR) | Feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Return** · build the habit | 2 (week 1), then 3 | 3 to 4 left in the tank | Easy, almost too easy |
| 5–7 | **Build** | 3, main lifts up to 4 | 2 left | Solid work |
| 8 | **Deload** | Half the sets, same weights | 3 to 4 left | Recovery week, still counts |
| 9–11 | **Push** | 3 to 4 | 1 to 2 left | Hardest block |
| 12 | **Consolidate** | 2 to 3 | 2 to 3 left; one top set on each main lift at RIR 1 to see progress | Celebrate, plan next 12 |

**RIR** means how many more reps you could have done. RIR 2 means you stopped with about two reps left.

**Starting weights (week 1):** about 60% of what you used to lift for the same reps. It will feel light. That is the point. Returning lifters regain fast, so weights will climb quickly in weeks 2 to 6.

## The sessions

About 50 to 60 minutes each including warm-up. ★ marks the 30 minute version: do only the starred exercises, with one less set.

**Warm-up (5 min, every session):** 3 min bike or rower, then 2 lighter ramp-up sets on the first exercise.

### Session A · Squat + bench

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Squat (back squat, or hack squat / goblet squat) | 3 × 5–8 | Leg press |
| ★ Bench press | 3 × 5–8 | Dumbbell bench |
| ★ Chest-supported row | 3 × 8–12 | Seated cable row |
| Romanian deadlift (light) | 2 × 8–10 | Back extension |
| Lateral raise | 2 × 12–15 | Cable lateral raise |
| Plank or dead bug | 2 × 30–45 s | |

### Session B · Hinge + overhead

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Trap bar or conventional deadlift | 3 × 4–6 | Romanian deadlift 3 × 6–8 |
| ★ Overhead press (dumbbell or barbell) | 3 × 6–10 | Seated dumbbell press |
| ★ Pull-up or lat pulldown | 3 × 6–12 | Assisted pull-up |
| Bulgarian split squat | 2 × 8–10 per leg | Walking lunge, step-up |
| Face pull | 2 × 15 | Reverse fly |
| Hanging knee raise | 2 × 10–15 | Cable crunch |

### Session C · Volume + arms

| Exercise | Sets × reps | Alternatives |
|---|---|---|
| ★ Leg press | 3 × 10–12 | Hack squat |
| ★ Incline dumbbell press | 3 × 8–12 | Incline machine press |
| ★ Seated cable row or one-arm dumbbell row | 3 × 10–12 | Machine row |
| Hamstring curl | 3 × 10–15 | Nordic curl (assisted) |
| Biceps curl + triceps pushdown (superset) | 2 × 12 each | |
| Farmer's carry | 3 × 30–40 m | Suitcase carry |

Each muscle gets trained about twice a week. That lands at roughly 8 to 10 hard sets per muscle per week in the build phase, which is where most of the gains are.

### Cardio session · Intervals on bike or rower

| Weeks | Session | Total time |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | 5 min easy, **4 × 2 min hard / 2 min easy**, 5 min easy | ~26 min |
| 3–4 | 5 min easy, **3 × 4 min hard / 3 min easy**, 5 min easy | ~31 min |
| 5–12 | 5 min easy, **4 × 4 min hard / 3 min easy**, 5 min easy | ~38 min |
| 8 (deload) | 30 min easy, conversation pace | 30 min |

**"Hard"** means 90 to 95% of your max heart rate: breathing heavily, able to say only a few words. This is the Norwegian 4x4 protocol, one of the best-supported ways to raise aerobic fitness (VO2max).

**Daily walking:** one interval session is less cardio than health guidelines ask for (150 minutes a week of moderate activity). Walking or cycling to work covers the gap. Aim for about 30 minutes most days. It needs no gym and no motivation.

## How weights go up

**Double progression**, the same rule on every exercise:

1. Each exercise has a rep range, for example 6 to 8.
2. Keep the weight until **every set reaches the top of the range** at the target effort.
3. Then add weight: **+2.5 kg** upper body, **+2.5 to 5 kg** lower body, the smallest available step on dumbbells and machines.
4. The new weight puts you back at the bottom of the range. Build up again.

| You logged (3 × 6–8, 80 kg) | Next time |
|---|---|
| 8, 8, 8 | 82.5 kg |
| 8, 7, 6 | 80 kg, aim for 8, 8, 8 |
| 6, 5, 4 | 80 kg, check sleep, rest times and technique |
| Below range two sessions in a row | Drop 5 to 10% and rebuild |
| Pain (not soreness) | No increase; swap to the alternative |

**Weeks 1 to 4 only:** if all sets beat the top of the range by 2 or more reps, jump 5 to 10% instead of one step.

**Rest between sets:** 2 to 3 min on starred exercises, 60 to 90 s on the rest.

## Low energy days

Slept badly or completely drained? Still go, but drop one set per exercise and take 5 to 10% off the first exercise. Swap intervals for 30 minutes easy. A light session protects the habit; skipping risks a zero week.

## Tracking until the app exists

Log in Hevy (free tier is enough): set up sessions A, B, C and Cardio as routines. Hevy shows last time's numbers next to each set, which is all double progression needs.

Every Sunday, 2 minutes:

- Did I hit the floor? The target?
- Which exercises moved up in weight?
- Book next week's two floor sessions in the calendar.

## When travelling

Hotel gym or dumbbells only: goblet squat, dumbbell bench, one-arm row, dumbbell Romanian deadlift, split squat, push-ups. Three rounds, 30 minutes. It counts toward the floor.
